Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026.

1. Who we are

Mir Financial Services Inc. (“we”, “us”) is an accounting and tax practice based in Ottawa, Ontario. We are the organization responsible for the personal information described in this policy. Questions can be sent to info@mirfinancialservices.com or +1 (613) 796 7030.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, contact details, and the content of your enquiry when you use our contact form or email us; and, if you become a client, the financial and identification information needed to provide our services (for example income slips, business records, SIN or business number, and government correspondence).
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as your device type and pages viewed, to the extent our website host records it in standard server logs. This site does not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and schedule a consultation.
  • To provide, administer, and bill for the services you engage us for.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and professional obligations, including tax filing and records retention.
  • To send updates about your service. We do not sell your information, and we only send marketing email if you have opted in.

4. Legal basis and consent

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario law. By submitting the contact form you consent to the handling described here. You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal and contractual limits, by contacting us.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share personal information only as needed to deliver our services, for example, with the Canada Revenue Agency when filing on your behalf, and with the providers we use for website hosting, contact form delivery, document exchange, bookkeeping, and email. We require our providers to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

6. Storage, security, and retention

We use administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including access controls and secure document sharing. We retain client records for the period required by tax law and professional standards (generally at least six years), then securely dispose of them.

7. Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask about our handling practices. We will respond within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The map on our Contact page is embedded from Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads; see Google’s privacy policy for details.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top reflects the current version.

Pricing

One fixed monthly fee. That is the whole model.

You get a fixed quote after the discovery call, based on your business, not an hourly meter that punishes you for asking questions.

  • One clear number every month, agreed before we start. No hourly billing.
  • Everything is in it: bookkeeping, your monthly financial statements, GST/HST, payroll and T4, T5, and T4A slips, your corporate tax return, and your personal tax return filed on time.
  • The guarantee. Your financial statements reach you every month, on time, or that month is free.
  • No surprise invoices. If the scope genuinely changes, we re-quote openly before doing the work.

What a quote depends on

  • Number and type of income sources or entities
  • Transaction volume for bookkeeping
  • Payroll frequency and headcount
  • GST/HST filing frequency
  • State of existing records
  • How often you want to meet

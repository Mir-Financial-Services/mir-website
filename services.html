Service 01

Bookkeeping & Cloud Accounting

Books that are current, reconciled, and actually readable, so you can make decisions on real numbers instead of a hunch.

  • Monthly bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online or Xero, reconciled and reviewed.
  • Financial statements every month. After each month closes, you get your statements before the end of the next month, so you always see what is happening in your company.
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations with a documented month end close.
  • Accounts payable and receivable tracking and simple approval workflows.
  • Clean up and catch up for books that have fallen behind.

Service 02

Personal Tax (T1)

Your return, done accurately and on time, with the planning conversations that actually lower next year's bill.

  • T1 preparation and filing for employment, pension, investment, rental, and self employment income.
  • Newcomers and departures, first Canadian return, residency questions, foreign income reporting.
  • Investment and property, capital gains, adjusted cost base tracking, principal residence rules.
  • CRA support, responses to review letters, adjustments, and prior year corrections.
  • Year round planning, RRSP, FHSA, and TFSA timing, income splitting, instalments, tuition and medical claims.

Service 03

Corporate Tax (T2)

Year end financials and corporate filings for businesses you own and run, with your personal picture kept in view.

  • Compilation (Notice to Reader) financial statements and T2 corporate return.
  • Remuneration planning, salary and dividend mix, RRSP room, CPP considerations.
  • Instalments and balances calculated and scheduled so nothing is late.
  • GST/HST and payroll year end reconciled to the financial statements.
  • Multi year view, loss carry overs, capital dividend account, and timing of large purchases.

Service 04

Payroll & GST/HST

The recurring compliance work that quietly causes the most stress. Set up once, then handled every cycle.

Payroll

Pay runs, direct deposit, source deductions, ROEs, and T4 and T4A slips, filed on the CRA's schedule, not yours.

GST/HST

Registration, return preparation, and filing on a monthly, quarterly, or annual basis, reconciled to your books.

Remittances

Calculated, calendared, and confirmed, with reminders before each due date so a notice is never the first you hear of it.

Service 05

Business Advisory & CFO Support

A financial partner in the room for the decisions that do not fit on a tax form: hiring, pricing, financing, growth.

  • Cash flow forecasting, a rolling view of what is coming in and going out.
  • Budgeting and targets you can measure against each month.
  • Pricing and margin analysis by product, service, or client.
  • Financing readiness, statements and projections lenders will take seriously.
  • Quarterly review meetings, what the numbers say and what to do about it.

Service 06

Business Plans

A clear, numbers backed plan for a new venture, a pivot, or a financing application. We do not handle incorporation or company registration, but we will build the plan that sits behind it.

  • Financial projections, revenue, costs, cash flow, and break even, built on assumptions you can defend.
  • Lender and investor ready format, the structure banks and programs expect to see.
  • Scenario modelling, best case, base case, and downside, so you know your margins for error.
  • Pricing and unit economics that hold up under questioning.
  • A working model you keep and update, not a document that goes in a drawer.
